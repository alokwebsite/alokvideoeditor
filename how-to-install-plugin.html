How to Install Plugin

Follow these simple steps for your OS

Windows

Step 1: Press Win + R to open the Run dialog.

Step 2: Paste the following path:

%AppData%\Blackmagic Design\DaVinci Resolve\Support\Fusion\Templates

Step 3: Press Enter and paste your downloaded Plugin files in this folder.

Mac

Step 1: Open Finder.

Step 2: Press Cmd + Shift + G.

Step 3: Paste this path:

~/Library/Application Support/Blackmagic Design/DaVinci Resolve/Fusion/Templates

Step 4: Press Enter/Go and place your Plugin files here.

Linux

Step 1: Open your File Manager.

Step 2: Press Ctrl + L to type a path directly.

Step 3: Paste the path:

~/.local/share/DaVinciResolve/Fusion/Templates

Step 4: Hit Enter and place your Plugin files here.
